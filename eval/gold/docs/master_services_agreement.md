# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of January 1, 2020,
by and between Northwind Consulting Inc. ("Provider") and Contoso Ltd. ("Client").

## 1. Term

This Agreement is effective as of the date first written above and continues for
a period of two (2) years.

## 2. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 3. Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days'
prior written notice to the other party.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall either party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceed
the total fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 5. Insurance

Provider shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than
USD 2,000,000 per occurrence throughout the term of this Agreement.

## 6. Audit Rights

Client may, upon reasonable prior notice, audit Provider's books and records
relevant to the fees charged under this Agreement no more than once per year.
