# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

THIS AMENDMENT NO. 1 (this "Amendment") is entered into as of March 15, 2001, by
and between Acme Holdings Inc. ("Company") and Globex Distribution LLC
("Distributor"), and amends the Distributor Agreement dated September 7, 1999.

## 1. Minimum Commitment

Section 3 is amended so that the Distributor shall purchase Products with an
aggregate value of not less than USD 1,500,000 during each contract year.

## 2. Governing Law

This Amendment shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
