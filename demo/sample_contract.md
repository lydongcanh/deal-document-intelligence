# DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

THIS DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of
September 7, 1999, by and between Acme Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation
("Company"), and Globex Distribution LLC, an Illinois limited liability company
("Distributor").

## 1. Appointment

Company hereby appoints Distributor as its non-exclusive distributor of the
Products within the Territory, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial
term of three (3) years. Thereafter it shall automatically renew for successive
one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at
least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Minimum Commitment

Distributor shall purchase Products with an aggregate value of not less than
USD 1,000,000 during each contract year.

## 4. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

## 5. Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days'
prior written notice to the other party.
